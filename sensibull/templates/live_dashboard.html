Alignment compares the two observed spot values only: PE selling with a rise, CE selling with a fall. It does not test Survivor's hidden reference points and cannot identify the strategy. No strategy is assigned to an individual trade from this statistic.

What can and cannot be concluded

Survivor sells puts after upward reference-gap breaches and calls after downward breaches. Analyse each underlying / expiry independently; checked-in defaults need not be active. Source references, resets, configuration, tags and complete fills are not visible here. Buybacks may be Wave, manual actions or other logic; inventory alone does not distinguish them.

A short observed delay does not prove that copying the signal is safe or profitable. Evaluate in paper replay at the first detection time plus additional delays, using independent option bid/ask history, fees, slippage, liquidity, margin and every hedge leg. This logger has no historical executable option quotes: it cannot calculate execution returns or validate timing insensitivity. It never connects to a broker or places orders.
